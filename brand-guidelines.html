02

Stroke Unification

The single most important alignment in this edition. As supplied, the icon was drawn ~10% heavier than the wordmark for its size — close enough to pass a glance, not close enough to be one family. Both marks now resolve to one shared stroke-to-height ratio, so a single stroke unit governs the entire identity.

1 : 11.3
Canonical ratio

The governing target — the true midpoint of the original two marks. Every new asset is drawn to it.

1 u
The stroke unit

One stroke width = 1u. Mark height = 11.3u. Bars, stems, arms and serifs all lock to 1u.

Sharp
Joins & terminals

Strictly mitred corners preserved throughout. The conform was done with a square kernel so no join was rounded.

Method: stroke weight was conformed on the artwork at 4× resolution, not faked by re-scaling the marks against each other in a lockup.

08

Misuse

The marks are conformed to a strict system. These break it. When in doubt, reach for an approved asset from /assets rather than redrawing.

Don’t

Don’t stretch or condense

Scale uniformly only. Distortion destroys the shared stroke weight.

Don’t

Don’t rotate or tilt

The mark balances on a vertical axis. Keep it upright, always.

Don’t

Don’t place on low contrast

Navy on mid-blue fails legibility. Respect the pairing matrix.

Don’t

Don’t add effects

No shadows, gradients, glows or outlines. The marks are flat.

Don’t

Don’t use off-palette fills

Only black, white, navy and gold variants exist. Use them.

Don’t

Don’t alter proportions

Re-deriving stroke or height breaks the 1:11.3 ratio.
